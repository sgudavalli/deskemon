# Baymax Companion

Static Codex pet made from the transparent PNG supplied by the user. The artwork is cropped to its transparent bounds and scaled proportionally; the illustration is not redrawn.

This version intentionally uses the same pose in every Codex state. It does not include walking, waving, blinking, or other frame animation.

Files:
- `pet.json`: Codex pet definition.
- `spritesheet.webp`: 1536 × 1872 transparent atlas with 192 × 208 cells.
- `baymax-artwork.png`: original illustration cropped to its visible bounds.
- `preview.png`: the pet at its app display size.

Installed under `/Users/prathameshpatil/.codex/pets/baymax-companion/`.
