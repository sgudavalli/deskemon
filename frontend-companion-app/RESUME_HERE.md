# Resume Deskemon here

## Build-day objective

Produce one polished physical-to-digital agent loop on a phone placed beside the laptop:

1. Deskemon visibly listens to a short physical conversation.
2. It extracts a commitment from speech.
3. It checks the commitment against calendar or active-work context.
4. It detects a conflict and speaks through GPT-Live.
5. It proposes an alternative and asks for confirmation.
6. It saves the approved commitment.

## Build order

1. Prove microphone input, speech detection, transcription, and GPT-Live speech on the phone.
2. Build the pet screen and its listening, thinking, speaking, and remembering states.
3. Implement commitment extraction and a seeded calendar-conflict check.
4. Implement confirmation and persistence.
5. Add action-oriented meeting summaries.
6. Add Slack presence if the core loop is stable.
7. Record the two-minute submission demonstration and preserve time for submission.

## Scope guardrail

The full vision includes camera presence, live Codex state, wellness behaviors, richer memory, and dedicated hardware. The hackathon demo succeeds when the single commitment-conflict loop works clearly and reliably.
